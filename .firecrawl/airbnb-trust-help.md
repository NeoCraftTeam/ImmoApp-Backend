# How Airbnb builds trust between hosts and guests

At Airbnb, we want to build the world’s most trusted community. To help ensure the safety of everyone, we dedicate a knowledgeable and experienced team to monitor any suspicious activity in our marketplace.

Airbnb offers several features that help build trust and cultivate transparency, including our secure messaging system, [reviews for homes](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/13), [reviews for services and experiences](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3863), [AirCover for hosts](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3142), and [AirCover for guests](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3218).

## **Report suspicious activity to keep everyone safe**

Help keep our community safe by reporting suspicious or inappropriate activity. To report something, click the flag icon ![](https://images.contentstack.io/v3/assets/bltec2ed8e3c4b1e16d/blt9046d62bdc5f778b/60f9dbc27d36d059ffd3ce23/flag.png) on **Profiles**, **Listings**, or **Messages**.

## **Resources for hosts and guests**

We strive to provide our hosts and guests with the right tools to make informed decisions regarding who they interact with on the site and in the real world.

Learn more about how Airbnb keeps our community safe by [verifying user information](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/336). And find our safety tips for [hosts and guests](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3063).
